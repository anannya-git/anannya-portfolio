02 / HOW I WORK

Clear thinking.
Thoughtful shipping.

I like the messy beginning of product work: the part where a real user need is still hiding inside a vague request, a surprising behavior, or an assumption no one has tested yet.

I bring discovery, strategy, and delivery together. That means getting close to customers, using data to understand what’s happening, and making the next decision legible to the whole team.

I’m at my best with curious, low-ego teams that move with intent: framing the problem carefully, learning quickly, and measuring whether the change helped.
